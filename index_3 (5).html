Currently Building

Generative Garden — an AI spatial generation product, in progress.

I'm co-founding Generative Garden — a product that turns years of spatial design expertise into a structured, rule-based system rather than a black-box model. The core product challenge: how do you take deeply tacit, expert-level design judgment and translate it into a set of computable, adjustable parameters that a non-expert user can actually work with?

We're starting with Japanese gardens as the first fully-parameterized use case — a domain precise enough to validate the rule system end-to-end. Working alongside researchers in spatial generation (CVPR / AAAI) on the underlying logic, while I focus on how that logic becomes a usable, controllable product experience. Prototype and case study coming soon.
